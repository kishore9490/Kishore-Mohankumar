The problem · 02

Three things break every time.

Even when you find vendors, the process falls apart at trust, comparison and negotiation.

Trust

Is this vendor real?

OEM partner levels, ISO and technical certifications, awards — all claimed on a PDF, none easy to verify. Buyers guess.

Comparison

Quotes nobody can compare.

Different formats, different assumptions, hidden terms on warranty, stock and payment. Apples vs oranges in a spreadsheet.

Negotiation

It vanishes into WhatsApp.

Counter-offers happen on calls and chats. No trail of who offered what, no proof of savings, no leverage next time.

And on the other side —

Vendors spend their days chasing opportunities they can't see.

?
win/loss visibility

Good vendors with ready stock and real partnerships have no efficient channel to reach buyers who need exactly what they carry — today.

Speaker notes This slide reframes the problem as three structural failures, not just "it's slow". Trust is unverifiable, comparison is impossible, negotiation is invisible. Then flip to the supply side: vendors are also losing — they burn time chasing leads and can't fulfil ready-stock demand they'd win instantly if they could see it. Two-sided pain sets up a two-sided network.
