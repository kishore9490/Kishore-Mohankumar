01 · Executive summary

A single, verifiable identity for every member of your workforce

Infinitia Links is a centralised digital identity, verification and workforce information platform. Every employee receives a secure, mobile-accessible digital identity card with a unique QR code. The organisation issues, manages, verifies, updates, suspends and deactivates those identities from one administrative dashboard.

Physical ID cards remain useful, but they are slow to issue, costly to replace and impossible to check for current validity. A printed card cannot tell a visitor, patient, customer or security officer whether its holder is still employed, suspended, or authorised for a particular role. Infinitia Links closes that gap: the QR code on each identity opens a secure verification page that checks the credential’s live status with the issuing organisation.

IssueIndividually or by Excel/CSV, on your own branded templates
CarryOn any smartphone via a secure link — no app required
VerifyAnyone scans the QR to see current, server-side status
GovernRenew, suspend or revoke centrally, with a full audit trail

What this proposal covers

  • Digital identity cards with six configurable templates
  • QR verification with active, suspended, expired and revoked states
  • Enterprise admin dashboard and credential lifecycle management
  • Bulk onboarding with validation and error reporting
  • Optional staff directory and staff service links
  • Sector use cases: education, healthcare, corporate, financial services, field workforce
  • A dedicated field executive and loan recovery agent module
  • Optional integrations: NFC, access control, SSO, HRMS/ERP, GPS
  • Security, privacy and governance approach
  • Deployment methodology, rollout plans and a commercial framework

Recommendation

We recommend a phased approach: a short discovery, configuration of branding and structure, a pilot in one department or branch, and a full rollout once the pilot is accepted. Pricing is provided as an editable framework and will be quoted after discovery.

Scope note. Infinitia Links manages identity credentials and their verification. It connects to — but does not replace — HRMS, payroll, attendance or ERP systems. QR verification confirms credential status; it does not independently prove the physical identity of the person presenting it.

04 · Solution architecture overview

A multi-organisation platform with server-side verification at its core

Experiences

Employees open their identity on a mobile browser through a secure link. Verifiers scan the QR code with any phone camera — no app or login is required. Administrators work in a web console with role-based access.

Platform services

The identity & credential service owns each credential and its lifecycle. The verification service answers every scan from the server, so a changed status is reflected immediately. The branding engine renders each organisation’s templates.

Data

Each organisation’s records are logically segregated. Photographs and assets are held in access-controlled storage. All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Backup frequency and retention are agreed per deployment.

Why server-side verification matters

The QR code contains only a unique, non-guessable reference — never personal data or a status flag. A screenshot of an old card therefore cannot show a valid status once the credential has been suspended or revoked.

07 · QR verification experience

Live status, in seconds, without an app

Verification workflow

ID presentedThe employee shows their digital (or printed) identity.
QR scannedThe verifier scans with any phone camera.
Secure URL opensA unique, non-guessable link for that credential only.
Status checked server-sideThe platform looks up the credential’s current status.
Approved details shownOnly fields the organisation has approved.
VerdictActive, suspended, expired or revoked — with a timestamp.

Verification page content

Verified / Not Active status · employee photograph · name · employee ID · designation · organisation · department · branch · validity · issuing organisation · verification timestamp. For non-active credentials, personal details beyond the minimum are withheld.

Safeguards

SafeguardDescription
Non-guessable identifiersEach credential link uses a long random reference, not a sequential employee number.
Server-side verificationNo status or personal data is stored in the QR; every scan is answered by the platform.
RevocationRevoked, suspended and expired credentials never display as valid.
Data minimisationConfidential employee data is never exposed; only approved fields are shown.
Anti-tamperingHTTPS only; rate limiting; tamper-evident page design; timestamped results.
Important: QR verification confirms the credential’s status with the issuing organisation. It does not independently prove the physical identity of the person presenting it — verifiers should compare the photograph.

ROI framework — transparent assumptions

We do not quote savings in advance. Instead, ROI is estimated with the customer using their own figures:

Annual card cost avoided = (employees × reissues per employee per year × cost per physical card) + (new joiners × cost per card)

Administrative time saved = (cards issued or updated per year × minutes saved per card ÷ 60) × loaded hourly cost

Net annual value = card cost avoided + time saved − annual subscription [To be quoted]

Qualitative value — reduced impersonation risk, faster verification and better auditability — is assessed alongside, not converted into invented monetary figures.
